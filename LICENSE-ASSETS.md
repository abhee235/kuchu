# Kuchu's name, character and sounds

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers Kuchu's source code. It does not cover these, which remain the property of their
owner, all rights reserved:

- the name **Kuchu**;
- the **desk robot character**: its design and look, the mark on its screen, its expressions and animations as a
  character;
- the **app icons**;
- the **sounds**, and the images and videos in `docs/`.

You may build and run Kuchu from this repository as it is, contribute to it, and show or write about it with
screenshots. A fork that is shipped to others needs its own name and its own character.
