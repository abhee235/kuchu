Every release is on [GitHub]({{RELEASES}}), with its files' checksums and build attestations.

## 0.1.6, 6 November 2026

- Kuchu now works on PCs without Visual C++. There, Claude Code showed a hook error on every message and the pet never
  moved.
- From the Microsoft Store, Kuchu comes back by itself after an update.
- The list of other sessions makes room for itself instead of cutting off its last card.
- From the Store, Disconnect also removes Kuchu's helper, so uninstalling leaves nothing behind.
- A flag in a conversation reports an answer from Claude.
- Settings show Kuchu's version.

## 0.1.5, 4 November 2026

- Five new things the pet does when there is no work: an arm goes limp, it droops, it looks lost, its head pops up on
  a spring, and it glitches.
- The pet waves hello each time you send Claude a message.
- Its thinking shows again: the eye up, the thought bubble and the loading ring.

## 0.1.4, 4 November 2026

- Woken from a nap, the pet starts, yawns and comes back to itself, instead of staying startled.

## 0.1.3, 4 November 2026

- A finished turn shows for a moment, then the pet goes back to its own life.
- The sessions window lines up neatly.

## 0.1.2, 3 November 2026

The security release: the pipe is your account's alone, the helper speaks only to your own Kuchu and passes on only
what a request offered, and the card shows all of a command or offers no Allow.

- Thinking in beats: the eye up, a thought bubble, an idea as the work starts.
- One look glides into the next, and the pet thinks between Claude's steps too.
- A card goes away once its request is answered in Claude Code's own window.
- Copies of `settings.json` are no more readable than the file, and only the last five stay.
- Release files carry build attestations.
- Kuchu runs as a Microsoft Store app.

## 0.1.1, 3 November 2026

- A Windows installer for one account. Kuchu's first start opens its Settings.

## 0.1.0, 2 November 2026

The first release: the pet and its moods, a card for each session with its steps, "+N" for the others, permission
requests on the card, conversations with Claude from the sessions list, a life of its own when there is no work, its
Settings, and starting when you sign in.
