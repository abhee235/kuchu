Open Settings from the tray icon (**Settings…**), or from your sessions list. Each change reaches the pet at once.
The sidebar shows which version of Kuchu you have, and at its foot, links to these docs and whether a newer Kuchu is
out.

## Pet

- **Your pet**: the robot, a cat, a dog, an owl, a frog or a rubber duck. Each does all the same things, with its own
  face.
- **Size**: Small, Base or Big.
- **See-through under the pointer**: Kuchu fades while your pointer is on it, so what is behind it shows. It still
  takes clicks and drags.
- **Plays when there is no work**: now and then Kuchu stretches, yawns, looks around, taps a foot, flips its screen,
  whistles, glitches, pops its head up or takes a short walk. After a few quiet minutes it naps; your pointer or a
  session wakes it.
- **Sounds**: a short tone when a session needs you, and when one is done or did not finish.
- **Starts when you sign in**: from your account's own list of startup apps. Kuchu is never a service. From the
  Microsoft Store, Windows starts it, and you switch that off in Windows' Settings, **Apps**, **Startup**.

## Beside the pet

- **What shows beside the pet**: **One line** (its name and its step), **A few lines** (its last steps, or the answer's
  first lines), or **Nothing** (Kuchu alone: its face says it all).
- **Keep an answer until you click Kuchu**: otherwise an answer shows for a few seconds, or as long as your pointer is
  on it, then fades.
- **Keep other sessions shown**: with several sessions at work, each stays on a card of its own beside this one.
  Otherwise they show while your pointer is on **+N**. A click on **+N** switches this too.
- **Clicking Kuchu opens**: your sessions, or Settings. **Ctrl+Alt+K** opens your sessions from anywhere, and the tray
  icon always brings Kuchu back.

## Claude Code

Connect Kuchu to Claude Code, update the connection, or disconnect it. See
[Connecting to Claude Code](/docs/connect/).

## Permission requests

- **Answer them on Kuchu**: when Claude Code asks to run a command or change a file, Kuchu shows it with Allow and
  Deny. See [Permission requests](/docs/permissions/).

## At the foot of the sidebar

- **Help and docs**, **What's new** and **Kuchu website** open these pages in your browser.
- **Updates**, from the Microsoft Store: Kuchu asks Windows whether a newer version is out, and says **Up to date**, or
  **A new version is ready** with **Update now**. Windows then closes Kuchu for a moment, installs the new one and
  starts it again. If Windows cannot tell, **Open the Store** goes to Kuchu's page there. Kuchu itself never goes
  online: Windows does the asking.
- **Updates**, anywhere else: **Check for updates** opens [What's new](/changelog/). Install the latest release over the
  one you have.

Kuchu keeps its settings in its own folder on your computer: nothing goes anywhere else.
