## The pet

The pet shows how your sessions stand, all together:

- **Thinking** while Claude reads your message or works out what to do next: its eye looks up, a thought bubble shows,
  and its pointer turns into a loading ring.
- **Working** while a tool runs.
- **Needs you** when Claude Code asks for permission or has a question.
- **Done** for a few seconds when a session finishes, with a happy face and a hop; or a frown if it did not finish.

It waves hello each time you send Claude a message. With no work at all, it has a life of its own, and naps after a
few quiet minutes.

## The card

Beside the pet, one card shows one session at a time:

- its name, as Claude Code titled it, and the project it runs in;
- what Claude is doing now. In **A few lines** (Settings, **Beside the pet**), its last steps, each marked: a turning
  ring while it runs, a green check once done, a grey cross if it failed or was cut short. What Claude said between
  steps shows too.
- when the session is done, the first line of Claude's answer, for a few seconds, or as long as your pointer is on it.
  To keep an answer until you click Kuchu, switch on **Keep an answer until you click Kuchu**.

Click the card to open your sessions. The **×** hides it until that session's next turn. On Windows, the arrow
button in its corner, **Open in VS Code** for example, brings the app the session runs in to the front.

## More than one session

With several sessions at work, the card shows the latest, and a round **+N** button says how many more there are.

- **Hover +N** to list the others, each on a card of its own, stacked on the main card.
- **Click +N** to keep them listed; click again to list them only on hover. Kuchu remembers this from one start to
  the next.
- **Click one of them** and the card follows that session from then on.

The list makes room for itself: above the card, or below it when the pet sits high on the screen.

## Hide Kuchu for a while

For a meeting, a game or a screen share, put Kuchu away for a while. Move your pointer onto the pet, click the small
button that shows beside it (**Hide Kuchu for a while**), and choose 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours, or 1 day.

Kuchu comes back by itself when the time is up, even if your computer restarted in between. To bring it back sooner,
click its tray icon, or press **Ctrl+Alt+K**, which also opens your sessions.

While it is hidden, Kuchu stays quiet: no cards and no sounds. Claude Code asks for permission in its own window, as it
does without Kuchu.

## Your sessions

Click the pet, or press **Ctrl+Alt+K** anywhere, for the list of all your sessions: what each is about, its folder,
and how it stands. From there you can also start a conversation with Claude
([Conversations](/docs/conversations/)) and open Settings.

Sessions go quiet in the list after 30 minutes without news, and are forgotten after 12 hours. Click **×** on one to
forget it now.
